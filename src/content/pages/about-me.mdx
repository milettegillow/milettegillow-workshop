My name is Dr. Milette Gillow. I'm a mathematician and opera singer by training (University of Leeds, University of Copenhagen, Royal Academy of Music). I've worked as a VC, university lecturer, and professional singer among other things, and I now run [The Tech Bros](https://thetechbros.io/): the world's first all-female, all-technical accelerator. I've organised 12 hackathons over the past year with The Tech Bros, but haven't built as much myself: so my aim for 2026 is to spend a lot more time developing. I love creative work, so I think I'll enjoy it!
